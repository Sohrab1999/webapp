> Greetings, Mr. Jamal Ahmadi,
>
> I am `[sender name]`. I am building a free, web-based educational application for English learning for Iranian students (grades 7–12), where students can practise lessons interactively (exercises, quizzes, flashcards). This message is a **permission request**; I have not yet used or published any part of your booklets anywhere, and I will not use them until I receive your written permission.
>
> I would like to request educational use of your three booklets:
> - "Vision One" (comprehensive grade-10 booklet)
> - "Vision Two" (comprehensive grade-11 booklet)
> - "Vision Three" (comprehensive grade-12 booklet)
> (third edition, Mehr 1401)
>
> If you are willing, please indicate exactly which of the following you permit:
> 1. **Digitization:** entering portions of the booklets' content as text/data into the application;
> 2. **Adaptation into interactive lessons:** reworking vocabulary, grammar points, and exercises into interactive activities and quizzes in the app;
> 3. **Web hosting:** placing the reworked lessons on the website (freely available to the public);
> 4. **Distribution:** allowing users to download or view lesson summaries;
> 5. **Commercial use:** do you want any restriction? Our proposal is that use of your material remain **non-commercial**, and if any commercial aspect ever arises for the whole application, we would ask you separately and in advance.
>
> Clarifying questions:
> - Which parts of the booklets are **your own original work** (explanations, tests, arrangement) and which are taken from the **official Ministry of Education textbooks (Prospect/Vision)**? I understand that rights in the official textbooks are not yours to grant and their status must be settled separately — I ask only to know precisely what falls within your permission.
> - **Attribution:** how should your name be credited in each lesson? (e.g., "Adapted from Jamal Ahmadi's booklets, 3rd ed. 1401" + a mention of your channel `@PracticalEnglishInKonkour` if you wish)
> - **Territorial scope and duration:** is the permission intended for internet-wide use (beyond Iran) and unlimited in time, or do you prefer restrictions?
> - **Restrictions:** do you permit transformation of the exercises into an interactive format, or do you want limits on editing/selection? Any part you would like excluded?
>
> I emphasize: this message is a request only; **no permission has been given and nothing has been published**. And if you prefer not to grant permission, please say so freely — in that case, instead of using the booklets' text, we will author our own equivalent original content guided by the lesson lists, and will still credit you as the inspiration for the lesson structure.
>
> With many thanks — `[sender name]`, `[date]`
